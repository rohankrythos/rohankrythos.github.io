AI Nutrition Engine – AI Calorie, Macro & Water Tracker (Closed Testing)

Fuel A Better You · Closed testing on Google Play

Log what you eat in seconds — type it, snap it or scan it — and see calories, macros and water at a glance.

AI Nutrition Engine (formerly NutriTrack Pro Max) is an Android nutrition tracker built for real everyday food, including Indian meals. Write “2 roti with dal” or take a photo of your plate and the AI estimates calories and macros for you to review and edit. Everything works without an account; sign in with Google only if you want backup and restore on a new phone.

Features

  • ✍️ AI meal logging from text, photo or voice
  • 🔎 Food search with a growing food database
  • 🏷️ Barcode scan for packaged food (Open Food Facts)
  • 🔥 Daily calorie & macro goals with a smart target
  • 💧 Water tracker with one-tap quick add
  • 📊 Weekly report and 7/30/90-day analytics
  • 🍽️ Saved “My meals” and recipe ideas
  • 📱 Home-screen widgets and gentle reminders
  • ☁️ Optional Google backup & sync, delete anytime

Frequently asked questions

Is AI Nutrition Engine available on Google Play?

It is in closed testing right now. Use the Notify Me form to hear about the public launch.

Do I need an account?

No. The whole app works without an account and your profile and meals stay on your phone. Signing in with Google is optional and only adds backup, restore and sync.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Backup & sync → Delete account & cloud data, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Is it medical advice?

No. Nutrition values are estimates. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet for a health reason.
