Habit Warrior

Delete your account and data

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Habit Warrior: Run & Habit (also known as Habit of a Warrior) account and the data connected to it. The App is developed by Rohan Krythos.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Habit Warrior and go to your Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Read the message and confirm Delete Account permanently.
  4. If asked, confirm it is you: enter your password (email accounts) or choose your Google account (Google accounts).
  5. Your account and data are deleted and the App returns to the sign-in screen.

This works for Google, Email and Guest accounts. You need an internet connection so the cloud copy can be deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the App (for example it is uninstalled or you lost the phone), email us from the address linked to your account:

appsupportwarrior@gmail.com

Subject: Habit Warrior account deletion
Include your Warrior name or Warrior ID and the email address (or Google address) of the account. We may ask you to confirm you own it. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account, including the email address and sign-in link.
  • Your cloud data in Firestore: profile, Warrior name and ID, avatars, habits, streaks, alarms, running history, XP, ranks, badges, missions, the purchase reference used to verify Elite Pro, referral and coupon records, leaderboard and weekly run statistics.
  • Server-side leftovers removed by our deletion function, such as your Warrior code.
  • The link between your Google Play subscription and your account, so a future account can use it again.
  • The same data stored on your phone (database, settings, cache, alarms, widget data).

What may remain, and for how long

  • Aggregated, non-identifying analytics and crash reports held by Google Firebase follow Google's own retention periods and are not linked to your deleted account.
  • Short-lived server backups and logs held by Google may take a limited time to be overwritten.
  • Google Play purchase history is kept by Google under its own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel an Elite Pro subscription: cancel it first in the Google Play Store (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) to avoid further charges.
  • Some records may be kept briefly for security, fraud prevention or legal reasons and are then deleted after the retention period.

Good to know

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Only want to leave this phone? Use Log out instead: your data stays safe in your account.
  • Uninstalling the App alone removes the data on the phone but not the cloud copy, so please delete the account first.
