Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DocToolkit – Scan & PDF Tools (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles your information. The App is developed by Rohan Krythos (“the Developer”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

In short:

• DocToolkit works offline. The only internet use is a one-time download of a translation language pack when you use Translate page.

• We do not collect, upload, sell or share your documents or any personal data.

• No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service.

• Your documents, scans and recognised text are never uploaded.

• No in-app purchases. The app is installed and updated through Google Play.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit or store any personal information on our servers. We do not have servers for this App. Everything you scan, import, create or edit — documents, photos taken with the camera, and text recognised by OCR — is processed and stored on your device only.

The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, microphone or advertising ID, and it does not require an account.

This matches the “Data safety” section of the App’s Google Play listing: the App does not collect or share user data.

2. Where your files are stored

  • Inside the App — your library (document list, favorites, folders, Recycle Bin, search index and OCR text) is kept in the App’s private storage on your device.
  • In your device’s public storage — documents you create or edit with the App are also saved as normal files in the Documents/DocToolkit folder (images exported from PDFs, and pages you save to the Gallery, go to Pictures/DocToolkit), so that you can open them from other apps and keep them if you uninstall DocToolkit. These files stay on your device. You can delete them at any time with any file manager.
  • Files shared to the App — when you use “Open with” or Share to send a file or photo from another app to DocToolkit (for example a photo to resize or a PDF to read), the App reads it from the other app and keeps a copy in its private storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Recycle Bin — deleted documents stay in the in-app Recycle Bin for 30 days and are then removed permanently. A document in the Recycle Bin is also removed from the Documents/DocToolkit folder.

3. Permissions we use

  • Internet — used only by Translate page, to download a translation language pack (about 30 MB per language) the first time you pick that language. No document, image or text is sent. Everything else works without internet.
  • Camera — used only when you scan a document. Pictures are not sent anywhere.
  • Storage / “All files access” (optional) — used to find documents that already exist on your phone (for example PDFs in Downloads or WhatsApp) so they appear in the App, and, on older Android versions, to save your created files into Documents/DocToolkit. Files are read only on your device and are never uploaded. When this permission is on, the App looks for new documents when you open it and from time to time in the background (a light, periodic check on your device). You can switch this permission off in system Settings at any time; the App will ask again the next time you open it, and you can still add files with Import, “Open with” and Share.
  • Storage write access (Android 9 and older only) — used only to save pictures and documents you create into Pictures/DocToolkit and Documents/DocToolkit on those older Android versions.
  • Biometric / screen lock (optional) — used only if you turn on App Lock. Fingerprint and face data are handled by Android itself and are never available to the App.
  • Secure screen (optional setting) — if you turn it on, Android blocks screenshots and screen recording of the App and hides its preview in the recent-apps list. It uses no permission and collects nothing.

4. Third-party services

The App contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device using Google ML Kit with models bundled inside the App; your images and text are not sent to Google or anyone else.

Translate page also runs on your device (Google ML Kit on-device translation and language identification). The text of the page is translated locally and is never uploaded. Only the language packs are downloaded from Google when you first choose a language; like any download, that request reveals standard technical information such as your IP address to Google, but not your documents. Once a pack is downloaded, translation works offline, and you can remove packs by clearing the App’s storage.

When you choose Share, Print or “Open with” another app, the selected file is handed to that app (or to the Android print service) at your request. What that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy.

Google Play and app updates. The App is distributed through Google Play. The App can ask Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if you agree, let Google Play download and install it. This is done by Google Play on your device and is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy; the App does not send your documents or personal data to Google for this. Google may collect its own information about installs and updates, as it does for every app on Google Play; we do not receive personal data from it.

The current version of the App has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If paid features are added in the future, payments would be handled entirely by Google Play, and this policy would be updated first.

5. Password-protected PDFs

When you lock a PDF, the password you enter is used on your device to encrypt the file (AES-256). We never receive your password and cannot recover it. If you forget it, the file cannot be opened.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data exists only on your device, so you control it. You can delete documents inside the App (they go to the Recycle Bin and are removed after 30 days, or immediately with “Delete forever”), delete the Documents/DocToolkit folder with a file manager, clear the App’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the App’s private data; files saved in Documents/DocToolkit remain until you delete them yourself. We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Your rights. Because we do not collect or hold personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, export or erase on our side. If you are in a region with data-protection laws (such as the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California) and want to confirm this or ask any question about your data, write to the contact address below and we will answer.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. The App does not collect personal information from users of any age. Parents who have questions can write to the contact address below.

8. Security

Because your files never leave your device, their security depends on your device. We recommend using a screen lock and, for sensitive documents, the App’s PDF password protection and App Lock. The built-in HTML viewer has JavaScript turned off by default and cannot access the network.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.
