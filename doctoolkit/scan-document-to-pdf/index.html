How to Scan a Document to PDF on Android (Free & Offline)

Your Android phone can replace a flat-bed scanner. With DocToolkit you point the camera at a page, the app finds the edges and straightens the picture, and you get a clean PDF you can share, search or lock with a password. Everything happens on your phone, so bills, ID copies and notes never leave your device.

Get it on Google Play

Scan a document to PDF with DocToolkit

  1. Install DocToolkit. Get DocToolkit from Google Play and open it. It is free, has no ads and needs no account.
  2. Tap Scan a Document. On the home screen tap Scan a Document and allow camera access when Android asks.
  3. Point the camera at the paper. Put the page on a flat, well-lit surface and hold the phone steady above it. DocToolkit detects the edges and crops the page automatically.
  4. Capture and adjust. Tap the shutter. If the crop is slightly off, drag the corners to fit the page exactly, then pick a filter that removes shadows and makes text crisp.
  5. Add more pages. Tap to scan the next page. All pages go into one multi-page PDF. Choose a frame (A4, square, 16:9 or 4:3) and the quality (high, medium or very high).
  6. Save the PDF. Save the scan. It appears in your Home and Recent lists and is also copied to the Documents/DocToolkit folder, so other apps can open it too.
  7. Optional: make it searchable, translate or lock. Use Extract Text (OCR) to copy the text, Translate page to read it in your language, or Lock PDF to protect it with an AES-256 password.

Tips

  • Scan near a window or under a lamp, and avoid your own shadow falling on the paper.
  • Place the paper on a dark, plain surface so the edges are easy to find.
  • Use the high quality setting for small print and the medium setting for smaller files.
  • For sensitive papers, lock the PDF and turn on App Lock in Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Does scanning need internet?

No. Scanning, edge detection and OCR run on your phone. Only the optional Translate page feature downloads a language pack once.

Where does my scan go?

Into your DocToolkit library and the Documents/DocToolkit folder on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
