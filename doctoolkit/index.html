DocToolkit – Free PDF Scanner, Merge, Split & OCR App for Android

Scan · Convert · Edit · Organize

Scan, convert, merge, split & edit PDFs. Fast, private, works fully offline.

DocToolkit – Scan & PDF Tools is an all-in-one document scanner and PDF toolkit for Android. Scan paper into clean multi-page PDFs, extract text with OCR, translate a page into your language, convert images and text to PDF, merge, split, compress, sign and password-protect files, and keep everything organized with search, favorites, folders and a Recycle Bin. All processing happens on your phone, so your documents never leave your device.

Features

  • 📷 Scan to PDF with camera, auto edge detection, drag-to-adjust corners, shadow-removal filters, multi-page
  • 🔎 On-device OCR: searchable scanned PDFs, copy text or save as TXT
  • 🌍 Translate page: read a scanned page or PDF page in your language (language pack downloads once)
  • 🧩 Merge, split (pick pages, every N pages or by size), compress with live before/after preview, crop PDF
  • 🔁 Reorder, rotate, delete and insert pages
  • 🖼️ Image to PDF, Text to PDF, PDF to JPG/PNG and PDF to long image
  • ✍️ Annotate, add text, draw, place and resize signature or image stamps
  • 🔒 Lock and unlock PDF with AES-256, optional fingerprint/face App Lock
  • 🗂️ Batch tools (rename, lock, compress many files) and Photo Resizer by pixels or KB
  • 📂 Home, Recent, Favorites, folders, full-text search and Recycle Bin (30 days)
  • 📄 Opens PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, TXT, CSV and HTML with a full-screen reading mode
  • 💾 Saves your documents to Documents/DocToolkit so other apps can see them
  • 🌐 Available in 6 languages: English, Español, Português, العربية, Français, Bahasa Indonesia
  • ⚡ Fast on big files, light on battery, works offline, no ads, no account, no tracking

What you can do with DocToolkit

Scan documents: use the camera as a pocket PDF scanner for notes, bills, ID copies, books and receipts. Auto-crop with adjustable corners, choose A4, 1:1, 16:9 or 4:3 frames and pick high, medium or very high quality.

Edit and organize PDFs: merge several PDF files into one, split a large PDF, compress it to share faster, crop margins, reorder or rotate pages, then annotate and sign.

Extract and translate text: turn scanned pages and photos into editable text with built-in OCR, copy it, or save it as a TXT file. Translate a page into your language right on your phone.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no analytics, and your documents are never uploaded. Lock PDFs with AES-256 and protect the app with fingerprint or face unlock.

Privacy Policy

Frequently asked questions

Is DocToolkit free?

Yes. DocToolkit is free to download and use. It has no ads and no account or sign-up.

Does DocToolkit work offline?

Yes. Scanning, OCR, converting, merging, splitting, compressing, signing and editing all happen on your phone. The only feature that uses the internet is Translate page, and only once per language to download its language pack. Your documents are never uploaded.

How do I scan a document to PDF?

Tap Scan a Document on the home screen, point the camera at the paper and capture. The app detects the edges and crops automatically, and you can drag the corners to adjust them. Scan more pages for a multi-page PDF, choose A4, square, 16:9 or 4:3 and pick the quality.

Can I translate a page of a PDF or scan?

Yes. Open a document, choose Translate and pick the language you want to read in. Translation runs on your phone. The first time you use a language, DocToolkit downloads a small language pack (about 30 MB); after that it works offline.

Can I merge or split PDF files?

Yes. Merge several PDFs in the order you choose. Split a PDF by picking pages (pages are saved in the order you tap them), by page range, every N pages or by maximum file size.

Can I compress a PDF or a photo?

Yes. Compress shows the original and the result side by side with the new file size before you save, and you choose to replace the original or keep a copy. The Photo Resizer sets exact pixels or a maximum size in KB.

Can I password-protect a PDF?

Yes. Lock a PDF with a password using AES-256 encryption and unlock it again when needed. The file itself is locked, so other apps ask for the password too. The password stays on your device. If you forget it, the file cannot be recovered.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Español, Português (Brasil), العربية, Français and Bahasa Indonesia. The app follows your phone language and you can change it any time in Settings.

Does OCR support Hindi?

Text recognition works for English and other Latin-script languages. Hindi and Marathi (Devanagari) pages can be read in the Translate page feature. Hindi text extraction in the OCR tool is not supported yet.

Where are my created documents saved?

Documents you create are saved in the app and also copied to the Documents/DocToolkit folder on your phone, so you can open them from other apps and keep them even if you uninstall DocToolkit.

Can DocToolkit edit Word, Excel or PowerPoint files?

No. Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX) and PowerPoint (PPTX) files open as text and table previews. PDF editing, annotation and signing are supported.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer.
