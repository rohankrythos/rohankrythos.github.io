How to Merge, Split and Compress PDF Files on Android

Sending one tidy PDF is easier than sending five loose files, and a smaller file uploads faster. DocToolkit merges, splits and compresses PDFs directly on your phone, without uploading your documents anywhere.

Get it on Google Play

Merge PDF files into one

  1. Open Merge PDF. Open DocToolkit, go to the Tools tab and tap Merge PDF.
  2. Choose the files. Select the PDFs you want to combine.
  3. Set the order. Arrange the files in the order you want them to appear in the final document.
  4. Save the merged PDF. Tap merge. The combined PDF is added to your library and to Documents/DocToolkit.

Split a PDF

  1. Open Split PDF. In the Tools tab tap Split PDF and choose the file.
  2. Choose how to split. Pick pages (they are saved in the order you tap them), enter a page range, split every N pages, or split by a maximum file size.
  3. Save the parts. Confirm and the new PDFs appear in your library.

Compress a PDF

  1. Open Compress PDF. In the Tools tab tap Compress PDF and pick the file.
  2. Check the preview. DocToolkit shows the original and the compressed result side by side with the new file size before you save.
  3. Replace or keep a copy. Choose to replace the original or keep both files.

Tips

  • Merge first, then compress, to get one small file to send.
  • Use Split by size when an email or form has an upload limit.
  • Compression keeps text readable; check the preview before replacing the original.
  • Locked PDFs must be unlocked first (Tools, Unlock PDF).

Frequently asked questions

Can I reorder pages after merging?

Yes. Open the merged PDF, tap the three-dot menu and use Add pages or the Manage pages tool to drag pages into a new order.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Merging, splitting and compressing happen on your phone.
