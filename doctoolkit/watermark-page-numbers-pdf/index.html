How to Add a Watermark and Page Numbers to a PDF on Android

Marking a PDF as CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT, or numbering the pages of a long document, takes a minute in DocToolkit. The watermark and the page numbers are two separate switches, so you can use either one or both, and you see a live preview of the first page before anything is saved.

Get it on Google Play

Add a watermark and page numbers with DocToolkit

  1. Open the tool. Open DocToolkit, go to the Tools tab and tap Watermark & page numbers, then pick the PDF.
  2. Switch on the watermark (optional). Turn on Watermark and type the text, for example CONFIDENTIAL. Set the opacity, size, colour and whether it runs diagonally. Any language works.
  3. Switch on page numbers (optional). Turn on Page numbers and choose the style (3, 3 / 12 or - 3 -), the position (bottom left, centre, right or top right) and the size. Skip the first page if it is a cover.
  4. Check the preview. The preview of page 1 is made by the same code that stamps the file, so it shows exactly what you will get. Numbers always stay inside a safe margin so printers do not cut them off.
  5. Tap Apply. DocToolkit builds the new PDF and asks whether to replace the original or keep a copy.

Tips

  • Use an opacity of about 20–30% so the text under the watermark stays easy to read.
  • Page numbers work on rotated and cropped pages and always appear upright.
  • A password-protected PDF must be unlocked first (Tools, Unlock PDF).
  • Keep a copy if you may need the clean version later.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use only page numbers, without a watermark?

Yes. Each feature has its own switch, and Apply works as soon as at least one is on.

Does the text of my PDF stay selectable?

Yes. The watermark and numbers are added on top of the existing pages; the original content is not re-drawn.
