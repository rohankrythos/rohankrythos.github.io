AI Nutrition Engine

Delete your account and data

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your AI Nutrition Engine account (Android package com.nutritrackengine.app) and the cloud data connected to it. The App is developed by Rohan Krythos. An account is optional: if you never signed in with Google, there is no account to delete.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open AI Nutrition Engine and go to Settings.
  2. Open the Backup & sync card.
  3. Tap Delete account & cloud data.
  4. Read the message and tap Delete to confirm.
  5. Your account and cloud data are deleted. The App keeps working as a guest.

You need an internet connection so the cloud copy can be deleted. The data already on your phone stays there; to remove it too, use Sign out before deleting, clear the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the App (it is uninstalled or you lost the phone), email us from the address of the Google account you used to sign in:

appsupportwarrior@gmail.com

Subject: AI Nutrition Engine account deletion
Write the Google e-mail address of the account to delete. We may ask you to confirm you own it. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including the e-mail address and Google sign-in link.
  • All backed-up cloud data: profile (including the name you entered), meal logs and estimates, water, weight history, My meals and synced settings.
  • Your daily AI usage records linked to the account.

What may remain, and for how long

  • Short-lived server backups and logs held by our hosting provider may take a limited time to be overwritten.
  • Shared food database entries (food names and nutrient values) are not personal data and are not linked to you, so they stay.
  • Meal photos are never stored by us, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Some records may be kept briefly for security, fraud prevention or legal reasons and are then deleted.
  • Anonymous usage counters from the automatic anonymous ID (used for the daily limit) contain no name or e-mail. If you want them removed, email us with the approximate install date and we will delete what we can match.

Good to know

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your data cannot be restored from the cloud afterwards.
  • Only want to leave this phone? Use Sign out: your data stays safe in your account and you can restore it later.
  • Uninstalling the App alone removes the data on the phone but not the cloud copy, so please delete the account first.
