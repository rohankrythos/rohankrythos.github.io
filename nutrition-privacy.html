AI Nutrition Engine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Nutrition Engine (the “App”, Android package com.nutritrackengine.app; earlier known as NutriTrack Pro Max) handles information. The App is developed by Rohan Krythos (Rohan Bairagi), Haryana, India (“we”, “us”). The App helps you log food, see nutrition estimates and track calories, macros, water and weight.

In short: the whole App works without an account. Your profile and meal history stay on your phone unless you choose to sign in with Google for backup. We show no ads, we do not sell data and we do not track you across apps.

1. Information stored on your phone

The following is stored only on your device (and is removed when you uninstall the App):

  • Your profile: name (optional), age, gender, height, weight, activity level and goals.
  • Meal logs, water logs, weight history, saved “My meals” and the nutrition estimates for each meal.
  • Settings such as units, language, region, reminders and theme.

The App does not allow Android’s automatic app backup to copy this data to other places, and the login session is stored encrypted with an Android Keystore key.

2. Information sent to our server when you use AI or food search

Our backend runs on Supabase. When you use a feature that needs it, the following is sent over HTTPS:

  • Food text you ask the AI to log (for example “2 roti with dal”) and, optionally, your region and language so the answer fits you.
  • A meal photo, only if you choose Camera or Gallery to scan a meal. The photo is reduced in size, sent to our server to be read by the AI and is not stored by us, neither in the App nor on the server.
  • Search words you type in the food search box. If a word is not found, we may keep the word and your country (never your name, account or full sentence) to improve the food database.
  • An anonymous ID that the App creates automatically on first launch. It is not linked to your name, e-mail or phone number and is used only to count the daily AI limit and keep the service fair and secure.

We do not store your food text or photos against your identity. Foods that the AI estimates (food name and nutrient values) may be saved in a shared food database that helps all users.

3. Optional account (Sign in with Google)

Signing in is optional. If you tap Continue with Google, we receive your Google account e-mail address and a unique account ID from Google. To provide backup, restore on a new phone and sync between your phones, the following is then stored in our cloud database: your profile (including the name you entered), meal logs with their estimates, water, weight history and these settings: My meals, calorie correction, units, language, region and reminders. Only you can read your data (it is protected by row-level security and encrypted in transit and at rest). We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it. This data is not end-to-end encrypted.

If you never sign in, your profile, meals and weight never leave your phone.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase hosts our backend and database (privacy policy).
  • Google Gemini API processes food text and meal photos to calculate nutrition estimates. Google’s API terms apply to that processing (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager) is used only if you choose to sign in.
  • Open Food Facts (open community database): when you scan or type a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product. No account or personal data is sent.
  • Google Play services: the barcode scanner screen is Google’s own scanner and the voice button opens Android’s speech recognition screen. They run on Google’s side; the App only receives the scanned number or the recognised text. The camera button opens your phone’s camera app; the App itself does not request camera or microphone permission.

5. Permissions

  • Internet – for AI estimates, food search, barcode lookup and optional backup.
  • Notifications – for the meal and water reminders you turn on. Reminders are created on your phone.

We do not access your contacts, location, files or messages. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or crash-tracking SDK.

6. How we use information

  • To calculate nutrition estimates and show your daily, weekly and monthly progress.
  • To provide optional backup, restore and sync.
  • To limit daily AI use, prevent abuse and keep the service secure.
  • To improve the shared food database and fix errors.

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use it for advertising.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Account data (e-mail, profile, meals, water, weight, settings) is kept until you delete your account.
  • Anonymous usage counters used for the daily limit are kept only as long as needed to run and protect the service.
  • You can delete your account and all cloud data inside the App: Settings → Backup & sync → Delete account & cloud data. You can also request deletion by e-mail, even if you can no longer open the App. Full steps: Delete your account and data.

8. Your rights and choices

You can use the App without an account, edit or delete any meal or profile data in the App, sign out, delete your account, or ask us by e-mail to access, correct or delete data we hold about you. Depending on where you live (for example India’s DPDP Act or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have additional rights; contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Health notice

Nutrition values, calorie targets and AI estimates are approximations for general information and are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet for a health reason. Do not use the App to diagnose or treat any condition.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security and international processing

Data is sent over HTTPS, protected by access rules on our database, and login tokens are encrypted on your phone. No system is perfectly secure. Our service providers (Supabase, Google) may process data on servers outside your country.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
