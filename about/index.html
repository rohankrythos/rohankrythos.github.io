About Rohan Krythos (Rohan Bairagi)

Android & Game Developer · Haryana, India

Real Name: Rohan Bairagi

I am Rohan Bairagi, known in the digital world as Rohan Krythos — a passionate developer who believes that every line of code is a step toward building something extraordinary. My journey began with a simple curiosity about how games work, and it evolved into a deep obsession with creating immersive digital experiences.

From Android apps that solve real problems to Unity games that transport players to new worlds, I blend technical expertise with creative vision to deliver premium experiences. Based in Haryana, India, I am constantly pushing boundaries — currently developing 3 new games and 1 new app alongside my existing projects.

My official projects include the warrior-themed productivity app "Habit Warrior" (live on Google Play), the step-tracking app "AuraStep", the epic 3D action game "Sword and Axe: 22 Thrones", and the upcoming nutrition companion "NutriTrack Pro Max". I don't just build software — I forge digital weapons for the modern age.
