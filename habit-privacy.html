Habit Warrior

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Habit Warrior: Run & Habit ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is developed by Rohan Krythos ("the Developer"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. The App is not directed at children under 13.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you sign in with Google, sign in with Email, or use an anonymous Guest account, we collect a unique user ID and, when available, your Google name and email address, to identify your account and sync your progress across devices. A Guest account can later be linked to a Google or Email account without losing any data.

Habit & Progress Data

We store the habits you create, your streaks, XP, level, missions, badges, Warrior profile, and related progress, to provide the App's core features.

Subscription & Purchase Information

If you subscribe to Elite Pro, your purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your card, bank, or other payment details. To activate and protect your subscription, we receive and store a Google Play purchase token and subscription status (active, expired, cancelled) linked to your account, verified against Google's Play Developer API by our backend (Google Cloud Functions). You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime from the Google Play Store.

Elite Trial, Referrals & Reward Tasks

The App offers ways to earn a temporary Elite Trial or in-app rewards without payment (e.g. War Path milestones, referrals, watching a rewarded ad, redeem codes). To prevent abuse of these free rewards, we may store your Android device identifier linked to your account. This identifier is used only for reward-abuse prevention, never for advertising or shared with third parties.

Location (GPS)

With your permission, the App accesses your device's GPS only while a running mission is active, to calculate distance, pace, duration, route, and leaderboard statistics. Your location is never sold or used for advertising.

Physical Activity & Motion Sensors

The App may read motion/activity sensors (step cadence, device orientation) during a run to improve distance accuracy and to distinguish running from other activities.

Camera & Photos

After completing a run, you can optionally attach a photo to your run's summary card — either by taking a new photo with your device's camera or choosing one from your gallery. This photo is processed and stored only on your device, used solely to create the summary image you choose to save or share, and is never uploaded to our servers.

Notifications

The App requests notification permission to deliver habit reminders, per-habit alarms, a daily morning summary of that day's habits, running-mission reminders, and reward alerts.

Alarm Data

Alarm and reminder schedules are stored locally on your device.

Leaderboard

If you take part in the leaderboard, your Warrior name, avatar, level, and running/habit statistics may be visible to other users.

Home-Screen Widget

The App offers an optional home-screen widget that shows today's habits and lets you mark them complete without opening the App. The widget only displays habit data already stored on your device or account — it does not collect, transmit, or create any information beyond what the App already has.

Language & Display Preferences

The App is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, French, German, Russian and Turkish. Your chosen language and theme (Warrior or Misty) are stored locally on your device; the App reads your device language and theme only to pick a sensible default. They are not sent to our servers. When you request a password-reset email, your app language may be passed to Firebase so the email arrives in your language.

Device & Diagnostic Information

We may collect device model, Android version, app version, and crash/diagnostic logs, to improve performance and fix bugs.

Advertising

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob. Elite Pro subscribers and users with an active Elite Trial do not see ads.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide habit tracking, alarms, reminders, and running features
  • Synchronize your data across devices
  • Process and verify Elite Pro subscription purchases via Google Play Billing
  • Prevent abuse of free trials, referrals, and reward tasks
  • Maintain leaderboard rankings
  • Prevent cheating and fraudulent activity
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Display ads to free-tier users

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored locally on your device (an on-device database) and, where cloud sync applies, in Google Firebase Firestore. Subscription verification is performed by Google Cloud Functions on our behalf, communicating directly with Google's Play Developer API. Run photos and widget content never leave your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & Firestore — account sign-in and cloud sync. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud Functions — server-side subscription verification.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases. See how Google Play works.
  • Google AdMob — displays ads to free-tier users and may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data. See Google's Ads Policy.

General reference: Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services required to operate the App (including verifying purchases via Google Play), public leaderboard data you choose to share, or when required by law.

6. Your Rights

  • Disable Location permission at any time, from Android Settings.
  • Disable Notification permission at any time, from Android Settings.
  • Remove the home-screen widget at any time, like any other Android widget.
  • Manage or cancel your Elite Pro subscription anytime via the Google Play Store.
  • Delete your account and data yourself in the App (Profile → Delete Account), or request deletion by email — see how to delete your account and data.
  • Uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally stored data.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained while your account remains active or until you request deletion, unless required by law. Subscription purchase tokens are retained only as long as needed to verify and manage an active subscription and to detect fraudulent or duplicate purchases.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
