Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in the game "Sword and Axe: 22 Thrones" ("the Game", "we", "our"). The Game is developed by Rohan Krythos ("the Developer").

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store or share any personal information from users, and the Game does not require an account. However, the Game uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you (see section 2).

2. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which may collect information such as device information, IP address and usage data:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements. See how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Unity Engine — the game development platform the Game is built on.

3. Advertisements

The Game displays advertisements through Google AdMob, including rewarded ads and interstitial ads. AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device's Settings (Google → Ads).

4. In-App Purchases

The Game does not include any in-app purchases.

5. Internet Usage

The Game itself can be played offline. An internet connection is required only to display advertisements, and ads may not work without internet access.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the revised policy.
