AuraStep

Small Steps, A Better You

A simple, battery-friendly step counter and pedometer that helps you walk more every day.

AuraStep: Step Counter shows your steps on a live progress ring, calculates distance, calories and active minutes, and keeps you going with streaks and gentle reminders. No sign-up is needed to start. Your steps are stored on your device and backed up to your account, so you can sign in later and restore them on a new phone.

Features

  • 👣 Live step count with distance, calories and active minutes
  • 🎯 Goal modes for losing, maintaining or gaining weight, with a daily step goal you can change
  • 🔥 Daily streaks with badges at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days
  • 📊 Calendar history, daily charts and weekly or monthly comparison
  • 🧩 Four home-screen widgets (4×1, 2×2, 4×2 and 4×3) that turn gold when you reach your goal
  • 🔔 Calm notifications: at most one nudge a day, quiet hours and a weekly recap
  • ⚡ Automatic low-battery mode or optional real-time tracking, using your phone's step sensor
  • 🌍 Available in 8 languages: English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Bahasa Indonesia, Türkçe and 日本語
  • 📏 Kilometres or miles, kilograms or pounds: set from your region automatically, or choose your own
  • 🔒 Works offline, with cloud backup so your steps survive a new phone or reinstall

Every step counts. Start your streak today.

AuraStep is a fitness and wellness tool, not a medical device. Values are estimates.

Habit Warrior

Build Habits. Break Weaknesses.

Build streaks, run with GPS and wake up with puzzle alarms – all in one RPG-style habit tracker.

Habit Warrior: Run & Habit (also known as Habit of a Warrior) combines a habit tracker, a GPS run tracker and a puzzle alarm clock into one self-improvement game. Earn XP for every habit and run, level up your warrior and compete on the weekly leaderboard.

The alarm makes you solve a challenge to switch it off, and it is built to keep working on phones with aggressive battery saving.

Features

  • 🏆 Daily, tick-based and unit-based habits with real streak tracking
  • ⭐ XP and 10 warrior ranks, from Recruit to Supreme Warrior
  • 🔥 100-Day War Path challenge and 15+ unlockable badges
  • ⏰ Puzzle alarm clock: Sword Slash, Shield Memory, Lightning Tap, Torch Maze, Archery and Crossbow challenges
  • 🏃 GPS run tracker with pace, distance and route, and automatic indoor/outdoor switching
  • 🥇 Weekly leaderboard with anti-cheat protected rankings
  • 👤 Warrior ID, custom profile and unlockable avatars
  • 🌍 Available in 8 languages: English, Español, Português, Bahasa Indonesia, Français, Deutsch, Русский and Türkçe, with a language picker in the app and automatic light (Misty) or dark (Warrior) theme on first launch
  • 👑 Optional Elite Pro: unlimited habits and alarms, no ads and cloud backup, with a free trial for new warriors

Ready to become a warrior? Start your War Path today.
