AuraStep – Step Counter & Pedometer with Streaks and Widgets

Small Steps, A Better You

A simple, battery-friendly step counter and pedometer that helps you walk more every day.

AuraStep: Step Counter shows your steps on a live progress ring, calculates distance, calories and active minutes, and keeps you going with streaks and gentle reminders. No sign-up is needed to start. Your steps are stored on your device and backed up to your account, so you can sign in later and restore them on a new phone.

Features

  • 👣 Live step count with distance, calories and active minutes
  • 🎯 Goal modes for losing, maintaining or gaining weight, with a daily step goal you can change
  • 🔥 Daily streaks with badges at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days
  • 📊 Calendar history, daily charts and weekly or monthly comparison
  • 🧩 Four home-screen widgets (4×1, 2×2, 4×2 and 4×3) that turn gold when you reach your goal
  • 🔔 Calm notifications: at most one nudge a day, quiet hours and a weekly recap
  • ⚡ Automatic low-battery mode or optional real-time tracking, using your phone's step sensor
  • 🌍 Available in 8 languages: English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Bahasa Indonesia, Türkçe and 日本語. It picks your phone's language automatically and you can change it anytime in Settings
  • 📏 Kilometres or miles, kilograms or pounds: set from your region automatically, or choose your own
  • 🔒 Works offline, with cloud backup so your steps survive a new phone or reinstall

Every step counts. Start your streak today.

Frequently asked questions

Does AuraStep work offline?

Yes. Your step history is stored on your device and no account or sign-up is needed.

How does AuraStep count steps?

It uses your phone's built-in step sensor, so you do not need a smartwatch. Choose automatic low-battery mode or optional real-time mode.

Does it have home-screen widgets?

Yes. Choose a 4×1 strip, a 2×2 tile, a 4×2 card or a full 4×3 dashboard. They show your steps and goal progress and turn gold when you reach your daily goal.

Will I lose my steps if I change or reset my phone?

No. AuraStep backs up your profile and daily step records to your account. Sign in on the new phone and your history is restored.

Why does AuraStep ask to allow background activity?

Android can pause apps to save battery. Allowing background activity keeps your step count complete and up to date, and the permission is optional. Even if you decline, AuraStep reads the full total from your phone's step sensor the next time you open it.

Can I use it to lose or gain weight?

You can choose a goal to lose, maintain or gain weight. When gaining weight, the step goal works as a limit instead of a target.

Which languages does AuraStep support?

AuraStep is available in 8 languages: English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Bahasa Indonesia, Türkçe and 日本語. On first launch it suggests your phone's language, and if that language is not available it uses English. You can switch language at any time in Settings.

Can I use miles and pounds instead of kilometres and kilograms?

Yes. Distance can be shown in kilometres or miles (Settings → Units) and weight in kilograms or pounds (next to the weight field in your profile). By default AuraStep follows your phone's region.

Is AuraStep a medical device?

No. It is a fitness and wellness tool and values are estimates. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise plan.
