Sword and Axe: 22 Thrones – Offline Fantasy Action RPG

War. Strategy. Glory.

Fantasy warrior RPG. Conquer kingdoms in offline battles.

Sword and Axe: 22 Thrones is an epic offline action RPG where courage, power and destiny collide. The world is broken, kingdoms have fallen and the 22 Thrones are under dark control. Only a true warrior can rise, fight brutal enemies and reclaim the lost kingdoms.

Play anytime, anywhere: no internet is required to play. The game is made to run smoothly, including on low-end devices.

Features

  • ⚔️ Fast-paced sword and axe hack-and-slash combat
  • 👑 Conquer 22 thrones, each one a fallen kingdom
  • 🔥 Boss fights with unique attack patterns that test timing and skill
  • 🛡️ Warrior progression: unlock new abilities and improve your combat skills
  • 🌍 Fully offline action RPG experience

Frequently asked questions

Can I play Sword and Axe: 22 Thrones offline?

Yes. It is an offline action RPG and no internet is needed to play. An internet connection is only used to show ads.

What is the game about?

The 22 Thrones are under dark control. You fight brutal enemies and bosses with sword and axe combat to reclaim every fallen kingdom.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

It shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Its privacy policy states there are no in-app purchases.

What is the age rating?

The Google Play listing shows an age rating of 12+ for fantasy violence and dark atmosphere.

You can read it on the Sword and Axe privacy policy page of this website.
