Habit Warrior – Gamified Habit Tracker, GPS Run Tracker & Puzzle Alarm

Build Habits. Break Weaknesses.

Build streaks, run with GPS and wake up with puzzle alarms – all in one RPG-style habit tracker.

Habit Warrior: Run & Habit (also known as Habit of a Warrior) combines a habit tracker, a GPS run tracker and a puzzle alarm clock into one self-improvement game. Earn XP for every habit and run, level up your warrior and compete on the weekly leaderboard.

The alarm makes you solve a challenge to switch it off, and it is built to keep working on phones with aggressive battery saving.

Features

  • 🏆 Daily, tick-based and unit-based habits with real streak tracking
  • ⭐ XP and 10 warrior ranks, from Recruit to Supreme Warrior
  • 🔥 100-Day War Path challenge and 15+ unlockable badges
  • ⏰ Puzzle alarm clock: Sword Slash, Shield Memory, Lightning Tap, Torch Maze, Archery and Crossbow challenges
  • 🏃 GPS run tracker with pace, distance and route, and automatic indoor/outdoor switching
  • 🥇 Weekly leaderboard with anti-cheat protected rankings
  • 👤 Warrior ID, custom profile and unlockable avatars

  • 👑 Optional Elite Pro: unlimited habits and alarms, no ads and cloud backup, with a free trial for new warriors

Frequently asked questions

What is Habit Warrior: Run & Habit?

A gamified Android app that combines a habit tracker, a GPS run tracker and a puzzle alarm clock. You earn XP, keep streaks and level up your warrior.

How does the puzzle alarm work?

To switch the alarm off you solve a challenge such as Sword Slash, Shield Memory, Lightning Tap, Torch Maze, Archery or Crossbow. Snooze limits stop an alarm from being ignored forever.

Can it track my runs?

Yes. It uses GPS and motion sensors for distance, pace and route, and switches between indoor and outdoor runs automatically.

Is Habit Warrior free?

It is free to download. New warriors get a free Elite Pro trial, and Elite Pro is an optional subscription billed through Google Play.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, French, German, Russian and Turkish. The App follows your phone language, and you can change it anytime in the in-app language picker.

Do I need to sign in?

You can sign in with Google or Email, or start with a Guest account and link it later without losing progress.
